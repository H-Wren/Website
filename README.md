## About

This portfolio brings together my work across international publishing, editorial practice, digital humanities, and AI-assisted knowledge systems.

Rather than presenting publishing and AI as separate career paths, the site follows a continuous interest in how knowledge is created, organized, preserved, and shared — with technology as the evolving medium.

## Design Philosophy

This website is designed as a digital publication rather than a conventional product landing page.

It uses editorial-inspired layout, paper texture, archive aesthetics, and library-card references to reflect a long-term interest in knowledge creation, preservation, and dissemination.
